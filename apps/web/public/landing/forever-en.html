Your Forever guild, organized without the headache

Dungeon runs, raids, the Race to 60 and characters kept up to date from the game. A website, an addon and a Discord bot working together.

What Forever Roster does

Free, no ads, made by players for players.

Dungeon runs

A group of 5 in a few clicks, with the guild's characters who have the level. Sign up in one click on the website or on Discord; in game, the addon offers to invite the group.

Race to 60

The guild's ranking since release, the podium of the first 60s and announcements on Discord, without typing anything.

The guild

The in-game guild roster, mains and their alts, who joined and left, who crafts what.

Raids and signups

One-click signup on the website or on Discord, recurring raids, reminders, assisted comp.

Loot

Soft reserve with SR+ bonus, or a loot council with votes, awarded in game by the addon.

Your characters

Gear, talents and professions updated from the game, with tooltips just like in game.

The addon

Quietly records the guild and your characters. One player pastes the website's data, the addon shares it with the guild. Tonight's runs, loot in game.

The Discord bot

Raid and run announcements with signup buttons, reminders by direct message, Race to 60 announcements, anonymous feedback.
