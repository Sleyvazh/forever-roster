What Roster does

Free, no ads, made by players for players.

Signups

On the website or on Discord, recurring raids, reminders and follow-ups for those who haven't answered.

Comp

Flex raids from 10 to 30, Mythic at 20: what's missing per role and who can fill it.

Roster addon

The loot master receives the items, hands them out by council or rolls, then trades them to the winner.

Loot council

Everyone answers BiS, Upgrade or Off-Spec, the council votes privately, the item goes out by trade.

Attendance

Attendance and loot recorded by the addon, each player's history.

Absences

Declared in advance: the raids concerned switch to "Absent" on their own.

The addon

Group loot: the leader picks it up, the addon handles rolls and the council, then the trade within 2 hours.

The Discord bot

Announcements with signup buttons, reminders and follow-ups by direct message, anonymous feedback.
